# Privacy Policy for URLMinerX

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026

URLMinerX ("the extension") is a browser tool for discovering and working with URLs on webpages. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how it is used.

## Data URLMinerX handles

URLMinerX reads the content of the page you choose to inspect and extracts URLs or related link information when you activate the extension. It may use:

- URLs found on the current webpage
- Link text and page titles needed to present extraction results
- User-selected export, copy, or filtering options

URLMinerX does not request or require account registration.

## Data storage and use

URLMinerX processes extracted URLs locally in your browser. Extracted data is used only to display results and provide features you request, such as filtering or exporting links.

URLMinerX does not sell, rent, or use extracted data for advertising. It does not use the data to build an advertising profile or track you across websites.

## Data collection and sharing

The extension does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. URLMinerX does not send extracted URLs to a developer-controlled server.

Data is shared only in the following circumstances:

- when you choose to copy or export data;
- when you choose to use or open a link or send a result to another service; or
- when required by law.

Any service you choose to interact with is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

## Browser permissions

URLMinerX requests only the browser permissions needed for its URL extraction and export features. Permissions are used for those features and for no unrelated purpose.

## Data retention

URLMinerX does not maintain a developer-controlled database of extracted URLs. Any data retained in browser storage, such as saved preferences, remains in your browser and can be removed through the extension or browser settings.

## Children’s privacy

URLMinerX does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's behavior or requirements change. The effective date at the top of this document shows the most recent revision.

## Contact
